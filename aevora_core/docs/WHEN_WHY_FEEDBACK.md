# Aevora WHEN, WHY and qualified feedback

## WHEN uses the same engine for every strategy

Inputs are scalar public runtime facts. Null, omitted, UNKNOWN and UNAVAILABLE
remain unknown; False and zero are known values. Episode names and strategy
names do not select an answer. A Rule is connected to actual Premise nodes with
allowed values. Its immutable premise declaration detects deleted/incomplete
relationship edges rather than silently dropping a condition.

APPLY requires a complete graph rule, known matching required facts, an actual
PASS support chain in the relevant verified topology, and no relevant known or
potentially relevant counterexample. It recommends a conditional principle,
with the evidence's limits, and does not promise a patch can be copied.

ABSTAIN follows a confirmed required premise conflict or a matching independently
verified business counterexample. VERIFY follows unknown required facts,
incomplete graph paths, unsupported current topology or an unresolved potentially
relevant counterexample. ERROR/TIMEOUT do not count as engineering counterexamples.
No numeric confidence or unobserved causal strength is produced.

The single process RLock evidence, the independent Worker failure and the unknown
topology fixture all pass through these same operations. Atomic SQL can cite
the separately verified A and D contexts. SQLite capability facts describe the
available persistence mechanism, not an assertion that the current candidate
already implemented a correct transaction.

## WHY is retrieved from FalkorDB

The public bundle contains actual Experience→Revision→Evidence→Outcome→Context
→Task paths and Revision→Rule→Premise paths, source category, stable Run/node
IDs, real verdicts and version fingerprints. It uses an explicit allowlist and
excludes private verification data, suite identity lists, maintainer paths,
reference patches and private applicability labels.

Its retrieval snapshot SHA256 is computed over the exact projected experience,
premise paths and outcome paths. Query/Decision bookkeeping alone does not
change the experience snapshot and cannot masquerade as new reliability
evidence. Retrieval is bounded actual Cypher reading; it is not claimed to be a
distributed transaction or third-party signed attestation.

## Ablations and limits

G2 tests run in UUID-named copies of the real G1 graph. Deleting a D support edge
or its final Task hop removes relevant complete support and turns APPLY into
VERIFY. Deleting one required condition edge makes the graph incomplete and
cannot strengthen a recommendation. Adding the actual historical RLock Worker
failure to a deliberately broad controlled-rule fixture changes VERIFY into
ABSTAIN; the fixture is explicitly synthetic, while its Outcome remains the
actual independently verified historical failure. The main experience graph
and historical OrderFlow artifacts are unchanged by these tests.

Unknown topology is a decision fixture; no D-v02 engineering implementation or
benchmark validation is claimed. H1 does not establish success-rate gains or
causal mental influence.

## FEEDBACK preserves separate facts

The Participant's query and Decision are observable tool use and an untrusted
adoption claim. They do not confer a VerifiedOutcome. The authorized host bridge
cross-checks OrderFlow sealed reports, patch/tree, suite/evaluator, JUnit and
direct database evidence. Only that independently obtained outcome can be
appended by trusted host code.

An outcome with insufficient causal attribution is recorded as
`outcome_observed_no_attribution`, UNKNOWN, with a new append-only revision and
OBSERVED_OUTCOME path. PASS alone does not improve the recommendation or prove
the experience caused the success. Subsequent independent Sessions can observe
the persisted evidence/revision difference while these causal limits remain.
